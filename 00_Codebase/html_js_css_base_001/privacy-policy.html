Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Reign of Play (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use the website at reignofplay.com and related online pages we operate. Individual products (for example mobile apps) may have their own privacy notices.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect:

  • Information you submit through contact forms (such as name, email address, and message content)
  • Basic technical data needed to operate the site (such as IP address, browser type, and request logs)
  • Any other information you choose to send us by email or other channels we provide

We do not require account registration to browse the public website.

2. How we use information

We may use collected information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide support
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the website
  • Understand how the website is used (via Google Analytics and Meta Pixel)
  • Protect against abuse, spam, and security threats
  • Comply with legal obligations where applicable

3. What we do not collect

The public website does not require sensitive personal information such as:

  • Home address
  • Government identification
  • Financial or payment information

Please do not submit sensitive personal data unless we specifically ask for it.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent personal information.

Limited data may be processed by service providers only as needed to host the website, deliver email, maintain security, or measure how the site is used. Those providers include Google (Google Analytics) and Meta (Meta Pixel). They are expected to process data only for the services they provide to us.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The website uses cookies and similar technologies for essential operation and to understand how visitors use the site:

  • Google Analytics. We use Google Analytics (Google tag ID G-KB1KD9243W) to collect information such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. Google may set cookies on your device and process this data according to its own policies. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Analytics opt-out.
  • Meta Pixel. We use Meta Pixel to measure visits and advertising performance. Meta may set cookies and process this data according to its own policies. See Meta’s Privacy Policy.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some site features.

6. Data retention

We keep contact messages and related records only as long as needed to handle your inquiry and for reasonable operational, security, or legal purposes, then delete or anonymize them when no longer required.

7. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, or loss. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

This website is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. If we become aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected unintentionally, we will take steps to delete it as soon as possible.

9. Your choices

You may contact us to ask about information you have submitted through the website, or to request correction or deletion where applicable and feasible.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the website after changes are posted means you acknowledge the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to us via the contact form on the website.

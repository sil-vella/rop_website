Privacy Policy – Dutch Card Game

This Privacy Policy explains how Dutch Card Game handles information when you use the Dutch Card Game mobile application.

Information We Collect

To operate the game, we may collect limited information, including:

  • A username chosen by the user
  • An email address chosen by the user
  • A password chosen by the user
  • Basic device information (such as device type and operating system)
  • Gameplay data (such as scores, game progress, and statistics)

This information is used only to provide and improve the game experience.

How We Use Information

The collected information may be used for:

  • Enabling mobile gameplay and multiplayer functionality
  • Saving game progress and player statistics
  • Improving app stability and performance
  • Preventing cheating, abuse, or technical misuse

Personal Information

Dutch Card Game does not require sensitive personal information, such as:

  • Home address
  • Government identification
  • Financial or payment information

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent user data to third parties.

Limited data may be processed by third-party services only when necessary for:

  • Hosting game servers
  • Basic app performance monitoring

Children's Privacy

Dutch Card Game is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

If we become aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected unintentionally, we will take steps to delete it as soon as possible.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect collected information from unauthorized access, misuse, or loss.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Li Ma Tagħmilhiex

A physical Maltese party card game from Reign of Play — built for groups, laughter, and nights that get a little chaotic.

About

Li Ma Tagħmilhiex is a tabletop party game in Maltese. Players draw cards and face whatever the deck throws at them — dares, questions, challenges, and twist cards that shake up the scoreboard.

Complete the prompt, earn points by level, and see who lasts when the room gets loud. Some cards ask you to act. Others make you answer. A few change the ranking mid-game.

At a glance

  • Format — Physical card game (party / social)
  • Language — Maltese
  • Play — Dares, questions, challenges, and special twist cards
  • Scoring — Levels and points on challenge cards
